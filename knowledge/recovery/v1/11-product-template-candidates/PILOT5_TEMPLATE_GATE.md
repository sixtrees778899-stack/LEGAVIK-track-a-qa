# Pilot 5 Product Template Gate

`SUPPORTED` means at least one APPROVED claim supports the fact. `REVIEWED` is internal candidate content. `CJAS` is unsourced or derived product guidance. `BLOCKED` cannot enter product.

## Binance — NOT READY

- APPROVED-supported fields: none.
- REVIEWED-supported: general security-control existence (AKI-BIN-001), stop concepts (006).
- CJAS Guidance: active handoff and record design (004/007).
- BLOCKED: lost email, Authenticator reset, post-recovery withdrawal state and operational estate path (002/003/005/008).
- Gate reason: critical recovery conditions and transfer-state fields have no APPROVED claim.

## Coinbase — READY FOR LIMITED HOLDER-RECOVERY MAPPING

- APPROVED-supported: lost email/2-step official recovery entry (AKI-CB-001).
- INTERNAL approved legal fallback: deceased document set, Executor Services and beneficiary limitation (002–004); manual escalation only.
- REVIEWED/CJAS: active handoff, record design and credential/authority warning (005/007/008).
- APPROVED time-sensitive: ID-recovery timing and possible sending restriction, limited to current CB-01 scope (006).
- Gate: holder-recovery fields may be mapped; legal fallback cannot be automated and timing must display its ID-recovery scope and review date.

## MetaMask — READY FOR CORE TECHNICAL MAPPING

- APPROVED-supported: SRP password reset, password/SRP distinction, derived-account discovery, imported-account recovery and SRP stop condition (001–005).
- REVIEWED/CJAS: address-check wording, legal authority and record layout (006–008).
- BLOCKED: none for core technical recovery; reviewed fields remain excluded.
- Gate: core facts may enter mapping; do not add legal claims or unsourced safety steps as official facts.

## Ledger — READY FOR CORE TECHNICAL MAPPING

- REVIEWED/CJAS: address/small-test and record layout (007/008).
- BLOCKED: unsourced composite guidance only.
- Gate: core facts may enter mapping; optional Recovery Key remains time-sensitive and model-scoped.

## Trezor — READY FOR CORE TECHNICAL MAPPING

- APPROVED-supported: backup formats, device-loss recovery, unrecoverable combination, Passphrase, threshold shares, phishing stop and Model One check (001–007).
- REVIEWED/CJAS: composite record layout (008).
- BLOCKED: generalizing Model One steps or backup format across models.
- Gate: core facts may enter mapping with model/configuration scope.

Pilot 5 as a complete product set is `NOT READY` because Binance has zero APPROVED claims. Limited mapping design may use only the explicitly eligible APPROVED items; no runtime integration is authorized by this report.
