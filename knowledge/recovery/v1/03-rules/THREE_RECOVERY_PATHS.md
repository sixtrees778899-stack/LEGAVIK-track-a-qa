# Three Recovery Paths

## Custodial platforms

### A — Holder normal access and recovery

The verified account holder uses ordinary login or official account-recovery controls. Completion requires more than page access: identity and transfer capability must be checked separately. This is the default recovery path.

### B — Holder-directed handoff or transfer

While capable and authorized, the holder initiates a lawful transfer or establishes an arrangement supported by the platform and applicable law. CJAS recommends owner-controlled transfer over sharing credentials. This path must record destination verification, restrictions and a small-test step where appropriate.

### C — Death, incapacity or inability to authorize

An authorized representative uses the platform's official estate/incapacity and legal process. It is a fallback, not default recovery. Login factors alone do not establish account ownership or legal authority. If no official process is evidenced for the applicable region, the answer is `UNCONFIRMED` and escalates to official support and qualified legal advice.

## Self-custody wallets

- Technical control: possessing the exact valid key material and satisfying wallet derivation conditions may enable signing.
- Legal authority: technical control does not establish ownership, beneficiary status or permission to move assets.
- Safe transfer: verify expected public addresses, network and destination; stop on mismatch; use a tolerable small-value test before material transfer.

Recovery instructions must keep all three concepts separate.
