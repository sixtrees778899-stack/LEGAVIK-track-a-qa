# Coinbase — legal

Approved claim projections in this domain: 4.

- **CBP-044** A Coinbase trusted contact cannot access funds or view the balance. (CBP-S07)
- **CBP-045** A trusted contact cannot transact or change the account. (CBP-S07)
- **CBP-046** A trusted contact is not automatically a legal representative or power of attorney. (CBP-S07)
- **CBP-047** Coinbase states individual accounts currently do not support naming a beneficiary. (CBP-S06)
