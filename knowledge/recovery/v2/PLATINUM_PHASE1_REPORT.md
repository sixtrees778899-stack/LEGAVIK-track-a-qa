## 5. Account access and security factors

The coverage matrix treats Login, Password, Email, Phone, Authenticator, Passkey, Security Key, Anti-phishing Code, Device Management, API Key and Address Book independently. A feature absent from official evidence is marked `NOT_PLATFORM_FEATURE_OR_EVIDENCE_GAP`, not inferred.

## 6. Operational and withdrawal control

All packages distinguish successful login from operational control and withdrawal capability. Security-factor changes, account/risk restrictions, allowlists, destination verification, regional information requirements and provider review remain separate control states.

## 7. Owner Verified Exit and Recovery Drill

The drill records access and factor checks, account restrictions, destination/network/Memo requirements, a small test, destination receipt, transaction hash and controlled completion. `Recovery Verified` cannot be set solely from sign-in success or a self-attestation.

## 8. Succession Passport

The passport records masked account identity, entity/region, factor inventory, recovery and exit references, legal escalation, recovery status, last verification and evidence hash. Passwords, OTPs, authenticator seeds, API secrets, full identity documents and real recovery materials are prohibited.

## 9. AI knowledge

Each of 300 offline FAQ records contains an Approved `claim_ref` and official `source_id`. Answers distinguish official summaries from CJAS Guidance. No AI service was connected and no free-form completion exists.
